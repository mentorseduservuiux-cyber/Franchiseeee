Mentors Eduserv Franchise

Your Pathway to Educational Excellence

Mentors Eduserv brings 14+ years of offline coaching experience along with online and hybrid coaching expertise to its franchise model. The institute has achieved 7,900+ JEE Advanced, 37,000+ JEE Main and 23,000+ NEET-UG selections, and is positioned as the No. 1 Coaching Institute of Eastern India.

As a franchise partner, you receive structured support across infrastructure setup, technology, recruitment, branding and marketing, training, curriculum and educational materials, and ongoing support and monitoring.

The academic system is supported from Patna through faculty support, annual and academic planning, test planning, class scheduling, student materials, review meetings and monitoring of academic operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mentors Eduserv is Eastern India's No. 1 coaching institute with 14+ years of experience, 7,900+ JEE Advanced and 23,000+ NEET-UG selections. The franchise model lets partners run a Mentors-branded coaching centre with full academic, technology, and marketing support from the Patna headquarters.
The franchise requires a one-time upfront fee of ₹25 lakh in Year 1. Revenue is shared 25% (Mentors) / 75% (Partner) on core tuition fees, and 50/50 on counselling and cross-sell services. Study materials are charged at ₹2,500 per student per year, and local ad spend is funded by the Partner.
You receive exclusive district-level rights for Grades 11–12, JEE, and NEET programmes. A Multi-centre Access Centre (MAC) can be opened beyond 30 km for Grade 10 and below courses. No other Mentors franchise will be authorised in your district for the core programmes.
The franchise agreement is for 5 years and is extendable on mutually agreed terms. Performance benchmarks and governance reviews (weekly CRM dashboard, monthly business review, quarterly academic audit, and annual commercial review) are built into the contract.
Mentors provides: central marketing and brand materials, CRM system and leads, student app and LMS, full curriculum and test series, teacher hiring support, school outreach, career counselling, performance dashboards, and a dedicated Relationship Manager for your centre.
